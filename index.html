Model 01

Studio Build

"I have an idea — you build it with me"

  • Our engineering team co-builds your product from scratch
  • AI-augmented development for faster MVP delivery
  • Shared equity with vesting milestones — both sides committed
  • Sprint-based delivery, joint review every two weeks
  • IP ownership clearly defined before we start
Best for: college students, corporate professionals, idea-stage founders
Model 02

Validation Sprint

"Test my idea before committing"

  • 8–12 week structured sprint before any capital commitment
  • Real customer conversations, market testing, feasibility check
  • Clear go / no-go decision at the end — based on evidence
  • Full investment available if validation criteria are met
  • No surprises — criteria agreed before the sprint begins
Best for: cautious founders, corporate innovation teams, market testers
Model 03

Founder-Led Investment

"Fund my business — I keep control"

  • Minority equity stake — you retain full operational control
  • Milestone-based capital — money released as you hit goals
  • Quarterly reviews with defined KPIs — no surprises policy
  • No board seat unless mutually agreed after Series A
  • Governance and reporting framework defined upfront
Best for: MVP-stage founders, entrepreneurs with traction
Model 04

Venture Partnership

"Fund and scale together — as full partners"

  • Capital investment with active strategic involvement
  • Defined board structure agreed before engagement
  • 18-month scaling roadmap with measurable milestones
  • Network access: hiring support, market entry guidance
  • Monthly operational and quarterly board reviews
Best for: revenue-stage founders ready to scale seriously

How we work

What happens after you apply — a clear, honest process

Our process reaches a clear decision within a defined timeframe. Transparency and speed are commitments — not bonuses.

01
Application review
Every application is reviewed by a real person. You will receive a response regardless of outcome. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you clearly and explain why.
Within 14 business days
02
Screening conversation
A 45-minute structured conversation. We assess the founder directly — not through the pitch deck. We ask honest questions and expect honest answers.
45 minutes
03
Technical & commercial assessment
Product review, market analysis, financial model scrutiny, and reference conversations. We go deep — so that when we say yes, we mean it fully.
1–2 weeks
04
Validation sprint (early stage only)
For early-stage applications: a structured 8–12 week sprint before committing capital. Market testing, customer discovery, and feasibility assessment — with measurable criteria agreed upfront.
8–12 weeks
05
Investment committee decision
Decision communicated within 5 business days of our committee meeting — with clear rationale. We don't ghost. We give you a real answer.
Within 5 business days
06
Structured onboarding
A 4-week onboarding: governance setup, reporting cadence, milestone documentation, and team integration. We start building on day one.
4 weeks
If we don't think we're the right fit after any stage, we'll tell you clearly and explain why. You'll never be left wondering — and we'll always point you toward what might serve you better.
